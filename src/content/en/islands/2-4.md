## Walkthrough

The **Earth Temple** is on Headstone Island, and getting in requires the **Command Melody** and controlling a statue warrior that clears obstacles for you.

- Before heading in, hit the switch on top of **Headstone Island** with a Hyoi Pear for Piece of Heart #38.

Inside, switch between Link and the statue warrior (controlled with the Command Melody) to solve pressure-plate and combat puzzles. The **Mirror Shield** shows up partway through the dungeon — essential against the Poes and the boss.

> ⚠️ This temple's ReDeads paralyze you with a scream — break the paralysis with the spin attack, or dodge them entirely with the Iron Boots.

## Boss — Jalhalla

Jalhalla is a boss made up of dozens of Poes flying in formation. Reflect light from a torch with the **Mirror Shield** onto its core to stun it, grab it, and throw it into one of the room's spiked pillars — this temporarily scatters the lesser Poes. Repeat until the core falls on its own.

## Items and hearts

- Piece of Heart #38 — switch atop Headstone Island.
- Piece of Heart #32 — the temple's own treasure chart, on Bomb Island.
- **Mirror Shield** — mandatory.
- Boss: **Jalhalla** → Piece of the Triforce of Courage.
