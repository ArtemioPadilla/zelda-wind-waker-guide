## Walkthrough

Before you can enter **Ganon's Tower**, you need all **eight Triforce Charts** deciphered and all eight Shards dug up. If you've been exploring throughout Act 2, you probably already have most of them — this is the moment to finish off whatever's left.

> ⚠️ Check the [Charts page](/charts/) and note which ones you're missing. Tingle charges 398 rupees each — make sure your Wallet+ is maxed out before the final round of payments.

Once complete, the Triforce of Courage assembles itself before the King of Hyrule. The reveal confirms what you already suspected: Ganondorf is after all three pieces of the complete Triforce (Power, Wisdom, and Courage) to wish Hyrule back to the surface, under his rule.

With the Triforce assembled and the Master Sword at full power, the King of Red Lions charts the final course: **Ganon's Tower**, at the heart of the most heavily guarded fortress in the Great Sea.

## Items

- All **Triforce Charts** must be deciphered and all **8 Pieces of the Triforce of Courage** dug up before you can continue.
