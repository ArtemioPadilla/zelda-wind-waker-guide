## Walkthrough

With the strength of all three Pearls, you return to the **Forsaken Fortress** — no stealth needed this time: the fortress itself recognizes your power, and the guards flee or stand down.

Explore the treasure room to get the **Power Bracelets**, which let you lift heavy boulders and statues — unlocking dozens of previously inaccessible spots across the Great Sea.

## Boss — Phantom Ganondorf (Dead Man's Volley)

Before you reach the girls' cell, **Phantom Ganondorf** challenges you to an energy duel: he hurls a dark orb you must return with a well-timed sword swing. Keep it bouncing between you and it gains intensity until he retreats. A chest appears with the **Skull Hammer**.

> ⚠️ Don't swing too early or too late — a mistimed hit damages you instead of deflecting the orb.

## Boss — Helmaroc King

At the top of the fortress, the **Helmaroc King** — the giant bird kidnapping girls on Ganondorf's orders — dive-bombs you. Dodge his charges until he jams his beak into the ground, then smash it with the **Skull Hammer**. After five hits, his armor is stripped away and he falls.

His mask shatters, revealing that **Tetra is actually Princess Zelda**, bearer of the Triforce of Wisdom. Ganondorf himself appears for the first time and takes her to Hyrule — the rest of the game revolves around chasing him down.

## Items

- **Power Bracelets** — treasure room, mandatory.
- **Skull Hammer** — reward for defeating Phantom Ganondorf.
- **Deluxe Picto Box** — available after this visit (upgrade it with Lenzo on Windfall).
