## Walkthrough

With the Power Bracelets in hand, it's time to head back to **Outset Island** to clear the **Savage Labyrinth**: 50 floors of underground combat, each tougher than the last, with no way to save partway through.

> ⚠️ Dying sends you back to floor 1. Go in with full hearts and every bit of ammo you can carry — there are no shops inside.

- **Floor 30:** a chest with a Triforce Chart/Shard.
- **Floor 50 (HD):** the **Hero's Charm**, which highlights nearby hidden items on the map — a real luxury for the rest of the heart hunt.

While you're at it, stop by **Stone Watcher Island**: use the Power Bracelets to lift the giant stone head and enter a mini-dungeon full of Darknuts, guarding another Triforce Chart. Outside, defeat the warships and cannons on the nearby platforms for an extra Piece of Heart.

## Items and hearts

- Piece of Heart #30 — Stone Watcher Island's platforms.
- Piece of Heart #44 — clear all 50 floors of the Savage Labyrinth.
- **Hero's Charm** — floor 50 reward (HD).
- Triforce Charts from Labyrinth floor 30 and Stone Watcher Island's mini-dungeon.
