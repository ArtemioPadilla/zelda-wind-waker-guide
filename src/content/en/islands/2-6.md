## Walkthrough

Scattered across the Great Sea are several **Great Fairy fountains**, each guarded by a stone guardian that wakes up as you approach. Defeat it (usually trivial) and the Great Fairy offers to upgrade your **Quiver** or **Bomb Bag** in exchange for rupees.

- First upgrade: 30 → 60 capacity.
- Second upgrade: 60 → 99 capacity.

On **Rock Spire Isle**, Tingle (once freed at the Forsaken Fortress) sells the **Wallet+** upgrade, essential for affording all eight Triforce Charts without running short on rupees.

> 💡 You don't need to seek out the fountains in any particular order — just visit them whenever you pass nearby during the Triforce Chart hunt.

## Items and hearts

- Piece of Heart #10 — for sale on Rock Spire Isle's shop ship, once you have the Wallet+.
- Piece of Heart #34 — chart bought on the same ship, leads to Southern Fairy Island.
- **Quiver+** and **Bomb Bag+** — Great Fairies.
- **Wallet+** — Tingle, Rock Spire Isle.
