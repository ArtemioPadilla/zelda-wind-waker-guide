## Walkthrough

**Ganon's Tower** is the final dungeon: several floors of pure combat against the toughest enemies in the game, without the environmental puzzles typical of earlier dungeons.

- **Double and triple Darknuts** guard the corridors — use the spin attack to crack their armor from behind or the flanks.
- Four separate chambers hide the **Poe Sisters**, each with a different combat pattern (invisibility, fake copies, teleportation). Defeating all four opens the way up.
- Save your healing items for the final stretch — there are no shops or fairy fountains inside the tower.

> ⚠️ Head up with your Quiver and Bomb Bag maxed out if you've upgraded them — the tower doesn't forgive running low on resources.

After clearing the combat floors, a final hallway leads to the throne room, where **Puppet Ganon** — a giant, remotely controlled marionette — blocks your path.

## Items

- Top off healing and ammo before continuing — a point of no return until the next checkpoint.
