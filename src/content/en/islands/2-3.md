## Walkthrough

From here on the game opens up completely: the goal is to gather all **eight Triforce Charts**, pay **Tingle** (398 rupees each, on his island-jail in the north of the map) to decipher them, and dig up the eight Pieces of the Triforce of Courage.

There's no required order — explore freely. Some key stops:

- **Rock Spire Isle**: Tingle is imprisoned here (you free him at the Forsaken Fortress later); there's also a shop ship with unique items.
- **Islet of Steel**: heavily defended by cannons and warships — sink everything with the Cannon before landing.
- **Private Oasis**: requires 20 Joy Pendants to enter; a mini-dungeon waits inside.
- **Needle Rock Isle**: a golden warship hides a chest on the seafloor — pull it up with the Grappling Hook.

> 💡 This stretch of the game concentrates most of the 44 Pieces of Heart — you don't need to hunt them all right away, but it's worth detouring whenever the route takes you nearby.

Buy the **Cannon** from Beedle's ship as soon as you have 200 rupees to spare — it solves half the naval fights in the game.
