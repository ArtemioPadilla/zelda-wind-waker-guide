## Walkthrough

With the Deluxe Picto Box and more tools in hand, head back to **Windfall Island** to finish off every sidequest you left half-done on your first visit.

- **Zunari's trading sequence:** start by buying the Town Flower and keep trading it for increasingly valuable items with the Great Sea's wandering merchants. Ends with a Piece of Heart and the **Magic Armor**.
- **Photograph Linda** with the Deluxe Picto Box and show it to Anton.
- **Maggie's letter:** mail it, then deliver the reply after your second visit to the Forsaken Fortress.
- **20 skull necklaces** to Maggie's father.
- Turn on the **ferris wheel** and shoot a **Fire Arrow** at its spinning top to reveal an invisible chest.
- **Decorate all 14 marked spots** around the island and talk to Sam on the bench.

> 💡 Water the **wilting trees** you come across on various islands of the Great Sea — each one hides a Piece of Heart.

While you're out there, visit **Five-Star Isle**: photograph Minenco hiding underwater to get her chart and a second Piece of Heart on the same isle.

## Items and hearts

- Pieces of Heart #7, #16, #17, #18, #19, #20, #21, #35, #42.
- **Magic Armor** — final reward of the trading sequence (halves damage taken in exchange for rupees).
