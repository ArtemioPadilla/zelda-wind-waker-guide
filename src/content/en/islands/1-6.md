## Walkthrough

With all three pearls collected (Din, Farore, and Nayru), the King of Red Lions carries you to the center of the Great Sea. Place them on their pedestals to raise the **Tower of the Gods**, the last trial before entering the sunken kingdom of Hyrule.

The tower is a floor-by-floor puzzle: bomb the guardian statues, shoot the light switches with your shield's reflection (once you get it inside), and time the spinning platforms.

> 💡 There's a Treasure Chart inside the tower that leads to two Pieces of Heart on other islands (Forest Haven and Pawprint Isle) — don't skip it.

## Boss — Gohdan

Gohdan is a two-handed guardian statue with a dragon head. Shoot the hands with arrows to stun them — if one grabs you, wiggle the stick to break free. With both hands down, shoot the head's red eyes: it lowers and opens its mouth. Toss a bomb inside to damage it. Repeat until it's defeated.

After the fight, the floor opens and you fall into the depths — welcome to the sunken kingdom of **Hyrule**.

## Items and hearts

- Piece of Heart #22 (on Pawprint Isle) and #36 (on Forest Haven) — the same Treasure Chart obtained here.
- Boss: **Gohdan** → access to Hyrule.
