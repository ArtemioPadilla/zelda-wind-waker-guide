## Walkthrough

The **Wind Temple** hides under the sand of a desert island, and reaching it requires the **Ballad of Gales**, which instantly warps you to any marked point on the map. Inside, **Makar** — the Korok you rescued in the Forbidden Woods — joins you; he's the one who can actually play the wind organ that solves most of the puzzles.

- Coordinate Makar with the Wind Waker to trigger air currents that move platforms and open doors.
- The **Hookshot** appears partway through the dungeon — you'll need it constantly against Molgera and across the rest of the Great Sea.

> 💡 After clearing this temple you can fly to Mother and Child Isles with the Ballad of Gales to get the **Fire and Ice Arrows** from the Fairy Queen.

## Boss — Molgera

Molgera is a giant sand scorpion that burrows and resurfaces by surprise. Keep your distance; the moment it surfaces, shoot it and Hookshot its blue tongue to pull it in and strike with your sword. The fight repeats in segments until it's fully defeated.

## Items and hearts

- Piece of Heart #28 — the temple's own treasure chart, on Thorned Fairy Island.
- **Hookshot** — mandatory.
- **Fire and Ice Arrows** — optional, Mother and Child Isles.
- Boss: **Molgera** → Piece of the Triforce of Courage. With this one, the Master Sword awakens its full power.
