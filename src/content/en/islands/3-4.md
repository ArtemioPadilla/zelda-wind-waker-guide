## Walkthrough

In the throne room, **Ganondorf** awaits you alongside Zelda, now awake as princess and fighting at your side with her own bow of light. The game's final scene unfolds in two phases.

## Boss — Ganondorf (phase 1: throne room)

Ganondorf attacks with fast strikes from his two swords — stay defensive, blocking and dodging, until **Zelda** lands a **Light Arrow** on him. That's your window: rush in and punish him with your sword while he's stunned. Repeat the dodge-arrow-attack cycle until the room collapses and the fight moves outside, atop the crumbling tower, in the rain.

## Boss — Ganondorf (phase 2: duel in the rain)

In the final stretch, Zelda fires Light Arrows straight at both you and Ganondorf at once — use your shield to **deflect them** into him at just the right moment. Every clean deflection stuns him for a sword strike. After enough hits, one last swing with the fully awakened Master Sword seals Ganondorf's fate.

> 💡 This final fight needs no new items — it's the culmination of everything you've learned: blocking, dodging, reflecting light, and the timed sword strike.

With Ganondorf defeated, the sea begins to swallow what remains of Hyrule by the King's own choice, leaving the past behind so the future of the Great Sea can rest in the hands of Link, Tetra, and whoever chooses to explore it.

## Items

- None — this is the game's final battle.
