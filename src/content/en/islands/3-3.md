## Boss — Puppet Ganon (phase 1: spider)

Puppet Ganon first appears as a spider-shaped marionette, hanging from strings in the ceiling. Shoot its single visible eye with arrows, or reflect light at it with the **Mirror Shield**, to knock it to the ground — rush in and finish it with your sword while it's stunned. Repeat until this form breaks.

> ⚠️ Its legs sweep in a wide arc — keep your distance while reloading or aiming.

## Boss — Puppet Ganon (phase 2: marionette curtain)

The second phase turns the room into a theater stage: giant marionette hands hold mirrors and fire beams of light. Use your own **Mirror Shield** to redirect those beams into the central eye that appears on the backdrop curtain. Hitting it opens a brief window to strike with your sword — repeat the cycle until it's defeated.

With Puppet Ganon down, the path to the throne room is clear. Zelda is inside, and Ganondorf awaits you for the final duel.

## Items

- None new — this fight is pure reflex and aiming challenge with the Mirror Shield.
