## Walkthrough

**Forest Haven** is guarded by wooden Koroks and the Great Deku Tree. Talk to the Great Deku Tree to receive the **Deku Leaf**, essential for gliding and for blowing wind into the boat's sail.

Before entering the **Forbidden Woods** (dungeon 2), explore inside the tree and climb the mushroom platforms with the Deku Leaf to find the hidden fairies.

**Forbidden Woods:** poisoned water raises and lowers the dungeon's overall water level — use the Deku Leaf to glide over it. You rescue **Makar**, the musician Korok, caged up inside, and get the **Boomerang** in the main room.

> ⚠️ The Forbidden Woods has one of the game's most disorienting rooms: the circular chamber with the toxic water. Use the map and clear it level by level instead of rushing straight for the boss.

## Boss — Kalle Demos

Cut the vines hanging from the ceiling with the Boomerang — the plant drops and exposes its head. Strike it with your sword until it closes back up, then repeat. Alternative: if you have Forest Water (found inside the dungeon) and pour it on its weak point, it dies instantly.

After the fight, you get **Farore's Pearl** and free Makar, who briefly joins you.

## Items and hearts

- Piece of Heart #8 — mailed to you by the Rito Chieftain after clearing this dungeon.
- Piece of Heart #36 — Tower of the Gods treasure chart, delivered here at Forest Haven.
- Piece of Heart #40 — the Forbidden Woods' own treasure chart, on the Angular Isles.
- **Deku Leaf** and **Boomerang** — mandatory.
- **Wind Waker** — gift from the three Forest Haven fairies once you find all of them.
- Boss: **Kalle Demos** → Farore's Pearl.
