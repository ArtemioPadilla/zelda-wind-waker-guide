## Walkthrough

**Windfall Island** is the game's hub town: this is where you shop, sell, play minigames, and kick off half a dozen sidequests. Come back often — several plotlines only progress if you swing by at the right moment.

- **Zunari** sells items by the dock and kicks off the trading sequence.
- Salvatore's house hides the **Sinking Ships** minigame (sink boats with the hand cannon).
- **Mrs. Marie** runs the hide-and-seek game against the Killer Bees.
- **Auctions** happen at night — bid against other customers for random items.

> 💡 Win Sinking Ships in under 24 shots for Piece of Heart #2. Talk to Mrs. Marie and win hide-and-seek for #3. The night auctions eventually put up a Piece of Heart (#4) — it can take several visits.

Also explore the interior: the café shop, Lenzo's photo house, and the school. Most of Windfall's full sidequests (the trading sequence, skull necklaces, the ferris wheel) need items you don't have yet — come back later.

## Items and hearts

- Piece of Heart #2 — win Sinking Ships in under 24 shots.
- Piece of Heart #3 — win hide-and-seek against the Killer Bees.
- Piece of Heart #4 — keep bidding at the night auctions until it comes up.
- This is also where you buy basics: potions, extra hearts in a bottle, arrows.
